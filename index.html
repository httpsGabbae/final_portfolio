03 — About

I'm John Aldrin Doruca, a second-year BS Computer Science student at Lipa City Colleges. Most of what I learn comes from building things, breaking them, fixing them, and figuring out why they work in the first place.

I enjoy turning ideas into working websites and applications, whether they start as a school requirement, a personal idea, or something I simply want to experiment with. Along the way, I've worked with different languages, frameworks, databases, and development tools while learning how each piece fits together.

I also enjoy exploring new technologies through experimentation and vibe coding. Sometimes that means starting with an idea, using AI to help bring it to life, then going through the code myself to understand, improve, and make it work the way I want.
